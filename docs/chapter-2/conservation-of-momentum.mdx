# Conservation of Momentum

If gravity and pressure forces are detailed, conservation of momentum in the $x$ direction yields:

$$\rho\frac{Du}{Dt} = -\frac{\partial p}{\partial x} + \rho g_x + S_{F,x}$$

In three dimensions, the equation becomes:

$$\rho\frac{D\vec{u}}{Dt} = -\nabla p + \rho\vec{g} + \vec{S}_F$$

After developing and rearranging the left-hand (material derivative) term, it becomes:

$$\rho\frac{\partial \vec{u}}{\partial t} + \rho(\vec{u}\cdot\nabla)\vec{u} = -\nabla p + \rho\vec{g} + \vec{S}_F$$

This is the **Navier-Stokes momentum equation** as used in this course: **local acceleration** plus **convective acceleration** on the left, balanced by the **pressure gradient**, **gravity**, and a lumped **friction/source term** on the right.
