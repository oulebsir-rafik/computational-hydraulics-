# Water Engineering Problems

- **Steady-state problems** in hydraulics involve systems that do not change over time, and their equations exclude time as a variable.
- **Propagation (dynamic) problems** focus on systems that evolve with time, and the goal is to calculate the state variables as they change over time.
- **Eigen-value problems** do not have a unique solution and aim to find multiple possible solutions; these problems can occur in both steady-state and dynamic analyses.
